Waterfront & event venues

People counting for waterfront and event venues

Measure attendance, flow and dwell across indoor and outdoor areas, so you can run events safely, size functions to demand and show partners what your space delivers.

Book a demo

The challenge

Several venues under one roof.

A multi-space waterfront venue is really several businesses at once, a public bar, a restaurant, an outdoor deck and bookable function rooms, each with its own rhythm. The bar can peak while the deck is dead; a function fills one room while the main floor empties. Weather and events make footfall lumpy and hard to forecast.

A single door total hides what managers need: where the people are, right now, by zone. Measuring flow per space is what lets you staff each area to its own curve and keep every zone within capacity.

What you can measure

The data you get from day one.

Live occupancy per zone

People in the bar, dining, deck and each function room right now.

Zone share

Each area’s share of total venue visitors.

Cross-zone flow

How guests move between entry, deck, bar and function spaces.

Dwell time

Pass-through versus destination, by area.

Function-room use

Visits per room and idle hours, booked versus actual.

Event uplift

Attendance and engagement for each function or event.

Capacity alerts

Warnings before a zone reaches its safe or licensed limit.

Conversion by outlet

Footfall against POS covers, per bar or restaurant.

Ways to use it

How venue and events teams use the data.

Staff each zone to its curve

Give the bar, deck and dining their own hourly rosters so staff follow the traffic between areas, not a single blanket plan.

Prove function space pays

See visits per room by hour to decide whether to keep, combine, re-let or re-price it, and show attendance against the number booked.

Route guests to high-margin areas

Use flow and dwell to check whether guests find and reach the rooftop bar or deck, and fix the path if they do not.

Hold each space within capacity

Track live occupancy per zone against its own safe limit, with alerts, so a crowded deck is managed before it is a problem.

Value an event

Compare footfall and cross-zone movement before, during and after an event against baseline to prove what it drove.

Compare venues

Use common zone definitions to benchmark bar, dining and event performance across sites and copy what works.

Business impact

Make more money, save money and improve experiences.

Make more money

  • Size functions, events and staffing to real demand, and sell space on evidence.
  • See which food, beverage and function zones perform, and price and promote them accordingly.
  • Lift high-margin areas by routing guests to the deck and rooftop bar.
  • Give sponsors and partners proof of the attendance and engagement they paid for.

Save money

  • Staff and clean each zone to its own pattern across indoor and outdoor areas.
  • Run HVAC, lighting and services to occupancy, not a fixed schedule.
  • Size kitchen prep and stock to forecast demand per outlet.
  • Plan layout and infrastructure to how the space is actually used.

Improve experiences

  • Keep crowds safe with live per-zone capacity and alerts.
  • Reduce queues and balance indoor and outdoor space.
  • Fix bottlenecks and wait points across a sprawling site.
  • Open the right space at the right time for a better guest experience.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Can you count across lots of separate indoor and outdoor spaces?

Yes. Sensors are chosen per area and zones are defined in software, so entrances, indoor rooms and open decks are all measured and combined into one live picture.

Can it hold each space within its own capacity?

Yes. Live per-zone occupancy is tracked against each space’s safe or licensed limit, with alerts as the limit approaches.

Can we prove a function or event paid off?

Yes. Attendance against the number booked, dwell, cross-zone flow and, with POS, spend per visitor, all compared to baseline.

Is guest data private?

Yes. Guests are counted anonymously, with no images or personal information captured.

How accurate is it for big crowds?

High accuracy is achievable at large scale, and we validate against your own records early so the numbers are trusted before you act on them.
