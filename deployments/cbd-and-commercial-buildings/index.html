Business impact

Make more money, save money and improve experiences.

Make more money

  • Give tenants data to value their space, and support leasing and rent reviews.
  • Monetise amenity, retail and event space by proving the footfall it gets.
  • Prove the return on lobby and amenity upgrades with before-and-after data.

Save money

  • Clean on demand, triggered by use rather than a fixed schedule.
  • Feed occupancy into HVAC and lighting so empty floors are not conditioned.
  • Right-size and consolidate space on evidence.

Improve experiences

  • Reduce lift and lobby congestion at peak times.
  • Size amenities and services to real demand.
  • Improve the tenant and visitor experience.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Can it integrate with our building management system?

Yes. Occupancy can be shared with building management systems such as HVAC and lighting controls, with integrations configured for you.

Can tenants see their own data?

Yes. Access can be limited by site and tenancy, so tenants see occupancy for their own space.

Is it anonymous?

Yes. People are counted anonymously, with no images or personal information captured.
