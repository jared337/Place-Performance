Measure how your building is really used, from the lobby to each floor, so you can run it on demand, give tenants evidence and prove the value of every space.

The challenge

Paying for space you may not use.

Real estate is most organisations’ second or third largest operating expense, yet hybrid work has broken the old assumption that heads equal desks. Office utilisation sits well below pre-pandemic levels, and leases lock in cost for years.

Measuring real occupancy, floor by floor and hour by hour, is the difference between paying for empty space and right-sizing a portfolio. It turns “the office feels quiet on Fridays” into the data that drives lease, consolidation, HVAC and cleaning decisions.

Ways to use it

How property and workplace teams use the data.

Right-size the portfolio

Consolidate or sublease under-used floors and model space needs ahead of lease events, the biggest cost lever of all.

Clean on demand

Service busy zones more and quiet zones less, instead of cleaning every floor on a fixed schedule.

Cut energy

Feed real occupancy into HVAC and lighting so empty floors are not conditioned.

Fix ghost meetings

See which booked rooms are actually used, and decide how many rooms of which size you truly need.

Set desk ratios

Set desk-sharing ratios and seat counts to measured attendance, and validate return-to-office policy.

Support leasing

Give landlords footfall and amenity-demand evidence for rent reviews, and tenants data to value their space.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Is it anonymous? Can tenants or staff object on privacy grounds?

No cameras identify anyone and no personal information is collected. Counting is presence and movement only, which matters for works councils, tenants and multi-tenant buildings.

Does it integrate with our building management system?

Yes. Occupancy feeds push to the building management system so ventilation, lighting and temperature follow real occupancy, which is where the energy savings come from.

In a multi-tenant building, can each tenant see only their floors?

Yes. Access is partitioned, so the landlord sees the whole building and each tenant sees only their own tenancy.

Can it tell occupied from merely booked for meeting rooms?

Yes. Sensing actual presence versus the calendar booking is exactly what surfaces ghost meetings and tells you how many rooms you truly need.

Does it measure zones, not just whole floors?

Yes. Zone and neighbourhood occupancy drives cleaning, layout and experience decisions, well beyond a single floor total.
