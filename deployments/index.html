FAQ

Questions & answers

Where does Place Performance operate?

Place Performance deploys and supports sensors across Australia, in metropolitan and regional locations, indoors and outdoors.

Can you count people at events and temporary sites?

Yes. We run temporary and single-event deployments as well as permanent installs, including pop-ups, expos and festivals, using battery, solar and mobile-data sensors where there is no fixed power or network.

Can sensors be installed outdoors?

Yes. Weatherproof sensors are mounted on poles and structures to count people, cyclists and vehicles along trails, streets and open spaces, in all weather.
