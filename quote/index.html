FAQ

Questions & answers

How is Place Performance priced?

Pricing is based on the number of sites and devices and the capability level you choose. Every quote covers the sensors, installation, commissioning, the platform and ongoing support.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

A rough idea of how many sites and entrances you want to measure, and which outcomes matter most. Exact device counts are confirmed during the site survey.

Is installation included in the price?

Yes. Every quote includes design, supply, installation and commissioning by our Australian team, plus ongoing support.

Can we start small?

Yes. Most organisations start with a pilot at one site and add sites and capability levels as they grow.
