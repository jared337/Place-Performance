About us

The team behind Place Performance.

Place Performance is a joint venture between two Australian companies: ITESA, specialists in measuring how places are used, and Rayven, builders of operational software and AI. Together we deliver the sensors, the installation and the intelligent platform as one service, designed and supported in Australia.

Our vision

Measure the place, not the person.

We help the councils, universities, governments and property owners who run Australia's public and shared places understand how they are really used, counting the place, never identifying the person, so every space can be made safer, run more efficiently and planned with evidence.

Measurement & analytics

ITESA

ITESA specialises in measuring how people, cyclists and vehicles use buildings, venues, parks, trails, streets and public spaces. They bring together sensing technology, edge processing, connectivity, data management, analytics and visualisation, and they design, supply, install, commission and support complete measurement solutions across Australia.
