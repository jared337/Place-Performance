Guide

What is a people counting and place analytics platform?

A people counting platform turns raw sensor counts into answers about how a place is used. Place Performance goes further than a dashboard. It covers the whole chain, from choosing and installing sensors to reporting, forecasting and AI recommendations, so the numbers you rely on are trustworthy from the first day.

From sensor to decision

Every deployment starts with a site survey. Our team looks at entrances, ceiling heights, lighting, movement patterns, power and connectivity, then designs a sensor plan. Indoors that usually means 3D people counters at entrances and over key zones. Outdoors it can include specialist sensors for pedestrians, cyclists and vehicles. Counting happens on the device, so only anonymous numbers are sent to the platform, as explained in privacy by design.

Organised the way you manage places

Data is structured as portfolio, site, space and zone. A council can see every library at once, then drill into one branch, one floor and a single children's area. Visits, live occupancy, dwell time and peak periods roll up automatically, so a regional manager and a branch manager look at the same numbers at different levels of detail. Live floorplans show each zone filling and emptying as it happens.

Insight, not just information

Dashboards tell you what happened. The platform also tells you what to do about it: threshold alerts before a room overflows, scheduled reports for managers and boards, visitation forecasts, scenario planning and a Place Assistant that answers questions in plain English. Capabilities are packaged into four plan levels, so you can start with counting and add intelligence when your organisation is ready.

Built for Australian public and shared places

The same platform serves councils, universities, government agencies, commercial buildings, shopping centres and outdoor public spaces. Each sector uses the same trusted data for different decisions, from crowd safety in a library to consolidating floors in an office tower.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What hardware does Place Performance use?

Place Performance uses commercial-grade 3D people counters for entrance and in-space counting, plus specialist sensors for cyclists, vehicles and outdoor areas where needed. Our team selects the right technology for each environment after assessing it.

Who installs and supports the sensors?

Place Performance designs, supplies, installs, commissions and supports the complete measurement system in Australia, from the initial site survey through to ongoing maintenance and expansion.

Does it work outdoors?

Yes. Deployments can be indoor or outdoor, permanent or temporary, covering buildings, venues, parks, trails, streets and public spaces, including pedestrian, cyclist and vehicle counting.

What systems does it integrate with?

It connects to point-of-sale, Wi-Fi analytics, building management systems, GIS, parking and ticketing platforms. Integrations are configured for you, and you control settings such as sync frequency and which data feeds reports.

Can I see data for a single room as well as a whole portfolio?

Yes. Data is organised as portfolio, site, space and zone, so you can drill from all your locations to one building, one floor and a single room, with every metric rolling up automatically.

How accurate is the data?

Accuracy depends on the sensor and environment, which is why each location is assessed and each device is calibrated during commissioning. Device health monitoring flags drift or outages so data quality stays high.
