Guide

What to expect from a demo and pilot.

A demo is a 30-minute walkthrough of the platform using data shaped like your own places. We focus on the questions you want to answer, whether that is crowd capacity in a library, utilisation on a campus or occupancy across a government portfolio.

The site survey

If a pilot makes sense, our team surveys your locations: entrances, ceiling heights, layouts, power, connectivity and privacy requirements. From that we prepare a sensor plan and recommend a plan level.

Your pilot

A pilot at one site is typically live within weeks. You get the same real-time dashboards, floorplans and reports as a full deployment, using your own data, so you can judge accuracy and value before rolling out further. Want to look around first? FAQ

Questions & answers

How long does a demo take?

A walkthrough takes about 30 minutes and focuses on your sector and the questions you want to answer.

How soon can a pilot be live?

After a site survey and sensor plan, a pilot at one site is typically live within weeks rather than months.

What information should we prepare for a demo?

It helps to know which places you want to measure, roughly how many entrances and rooms they have, and the decisions you want the data to support. Floorplans are useful but not required for the first conversation.

Is there any obligation after a demo?

No. A demo is a walkthrough to see whether Place Performance fits your needs. If it does, we scope a pilot together.

Do you work outside major cities?

Yes. Solutions are designed and supported for Australian conditions, from busy urban spaces to parks, trails, coastal and remote locations.
