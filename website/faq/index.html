FAQ

Questions & answers.

Everything you need to know about people counting, occupancy monitoring, space utilisation and AI insights with Place Performance.

General

What is Place Performance?

Place Performance is an end-to-end people counting and place analytics service. It combines privacy-safe sensors, professional installation, a real-time analytics platform and AI insights so councils, universities, government and property owners can see how their places are used and plan with evidence.

What does Place Performance measure?

It measures visitor counts, entries and exits, live occupancy against capacity, peak periods, dwell time, repeat visitation, movement between zones and, outdoors, pedestrians, cyclists and vehicles. Metrics roll up from a single room to a whole portfolio of sites.

Is the hardware included?

Yes. FootfallCam 3D people counters and other sensors are selected for each environment, then designed, supplied, installed, commissioned and supported by Place Performance in Australia, so you deal with one partner from sensor to insight.

Does it identify people?

No. Counting happens on the device and data is masked at source, so only anonymous numbers leave the site. The approach is to measure the place, not the person. See Privacy by design.

Who is it for?

It is built for local councils, universities and TAFEs, state and federal government agencies, commercial property owners, shopping centres and precinct managers, and anyone responsible for libraries, civic centres, venues, parks, trails or streets.

How quickly can we get started?

Most organisations start with a pilot at one site. After a site survey and sensor plan, a pilot is typically live in weeks rather than months, and you can add sites and capability levels as you grow.

Platform & hardware

What hardware does Place Performance use?

Place Performance uses FootfallCam 3D people counters for entrance and in-space counting, plus specialist sensors for cyclists, vehicles and outdoor areas where needed. Our team selects the right technology for each environment after assessing it.

Who installs and supports the sensors?

Place Performance designs, supplies, installs, commissions and supports the complete measurement system in Australia, from the initial site survey through to ongoing maintenance and expansion.

Does it work outdoors?

Yes. Deployments can be indoor or outdoor, permanent or temporary, covering buildings, venues, parks, trails, streets and public spaces, including pedestrian, cyclist and vehicle counting.

What systems does it integrate with?

It connects to point-of-sale, Wi-Fi analytics, building management systems, GIS, parking and ticketing platforms. Integrations are configured for you, and you control settings such as sync frequency and which data feeds reports.

Can I see data for a single room as well as a whole portfolio?

Yes. Data is organised as portfolio, site, space and zone, so you can drill from all your locations to one building, one floor and a single room, with every metric rolling up automatically.

How accurate is the data?

Accuracy depends on the sensor and environment, which is why each location is assessed and each device is calibrated during commissioning. Device health monitoring flags drift or outages so data quality stays high.

Real time & alerts

How often does occupancy data update?

Live occupancy refreshes every few seconds on dashboards and floorplans, and sensors typically report counts every minute. Historical views aggregate the same data by hour, day, week, month and year.

What alerts can I set up?

You can alert on occupancy above a percentage of capacity, queue wait times, entry and exit imbalances, sensor heartbeat loss, integration sync failures and unusual activity compared with what is expected, each with its own duration, scope and severity.

How are notifications delivered?

Notifications can be sent by email, SMS, Microsoft Teams or in-app, to individuals or teams, with optional escalation to another person if an alert is not acknowledged in time.

Can I see live occupancy on a floorplan?

Yes. You upload a floorplan for each floor, draw each room or zone as a shape, and every zone then shows its live headcount and is coloured by how full it is.

What happens if a sensor goes offline?

Each device sends a regular heartbeat. If it stops, an alert is raised, the device is flagged on your floorplan and device list, and the support team is notified so it can be fixed quickly.

Can I monitor crowd capacity at events?

Yes. Temporary counting can be deployed for events to track attendance, entry flow and live crowd numbers against venue limits, with alerts before limits are reached.

AI & forecasting

How does visitation forecasting work?

Forecasts are built from each place's own history, including time of day, day of week and seasonal patterns, to predict visitation and occupancy for the weeks ahead. Each forecast includes a confidence range that widens further into the future.

What can I ask the Place Assistant?

You can ask questions such as which rooms are over capacity, when a site is busiest, which sensors are offline, how this week compares with last, or what to focus on. Answers use your live data and link directly to the places they mention.

Which AI model powers the Place Assistant?

The Place Assistant uses Claude, a large language model from Anthropic, given a snapshot of the data your role is allowed to see at the time you ask. Contact us for details of data handling for your organisation.

What is scenario planning?

Scenario planning lets you change assumptions such as opening hours, capacity, staffing or layout and immediately see the projected effect on visits, peak occupancy, queue times and cost per visit, then save and compare scenarios side by side.

How are AI insights generated?

Each morning the platform analyses trends, peaks, capacity breaches and comparisons across your sites and writes plain-language recommendations ranked by impact, each with the supporting evidence and a confidence score.

Can it help justify capital investment?

Yes. Forecasts support the business case before you build, and investment analysis compares visitation, dwell and other outcomes before and after a project to show whether it paid off.

Plans & pricing

What is included in every plan?

Every plan includes the sensors, site survey, installation, commissioning, the analytics platform, device health monitoring and ongoing support. Higher levels add capabilities on top of everything in the levels below.

How is Place Performance priced?

Pricing is based on the number of sites and devices and the capability level you choose. Contact us for a quote tailored to your locations.

Can we start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Many organisations start with Essentials at one or a few sites and move up a level when they are ready. Upgrades add features without replacing hardware or losing history.

Which level do we need for multiple sites?

Multi-site portfolio views, site ranking and outlier flags start at Professional (Level 3). Portfolio-wide performance index and external benchmarking are part of Executive (Level 4).

Which level includes AI and forecasting?

AI insights, the Place Assistant, forecasting, scenario modelling, investment analysis and board reporting packs are included in Executive (Level 4).

Can we run a pilot first?

Yes. A pilot at one site is the most common way to start. It proves accuracy and value with your own data before you commit to a wider rollout.

Privacy

Do the sensors record video?

No video is streamed to or stored by the platform. The sensors process what they see on the device and send only anonymous counts and occupancy numbers.

Can people be identified?

No. Data is masked at source and no personal information such as faces or names is collected. The system measures how places are used, not who uses them.

Does Place Performance use facial recognition?

No. Place Performance does not use facial recognition and does not identify individuals.

What data is collected?

Counts of people entering and leaving, occupancy, dwell time, movement between zones and, where enabled and supported, anonymous demographic estimates. Outdoors it can also count cyclists and vehicles.

Who can access the data?

Only users in your organisation with an assigned role. Access can be limited to specific sites, single sign-on can be enforced, and administrative actions are recorded in an audit log.

Does it support Australian privacy obligations?

Anonymous, edge-processed counting with minimal data collection is designed to support organisations' obligations under Australian privacy law. Contact us for documentation to support your privacy impact assessment.
