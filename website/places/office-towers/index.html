Why measure

Hybrid work changed the office. Measure the new normal.

Office attendance now varies by day, team and floor. Building owners and tenants need to know how space is really used to make good decisions on cleaning, energy, amenities and leasing.

Floors, rooms and amenities

Sensors measure occupancy on each floor and in meeting rooms, kitchens and end-of-trip facilities. Comparing booked with actual use reveals rooms that are reserved but empty, and attendance by weekday shows hybrid patterns.

Operate on demand

Occupancy shared with the building management system lets HVAC and lighting respond to real use, and demand-based cleaning is triggered by visits. Read more about occupancy analytics for commercial buildings.

Evidence for tenants and owners

Tenants can see their own space, supporting workplace strategy and lease decisions, while owners use investment analysis to prove the value of refits. Government agencies use the same data to right-size their office portfolios.

Typical uses in office towers

Facilities managers trigger cleaning by use, engineers feed occupancy into HVAC controls, workplace strategists study hybrid attendance, leasing teams give tenants evidence about their space, and asset managers measure the effect of lobby and amenity upgrades.

FAQ

Questions & answers

How is office occupancy measured?

Ceiling-mounted sensors count people entering floors and zones and measure how many are present, giving live and historical occupancy per floor, room and amenity.

Can it show hybrid work attendance patterns?

Yes. Occupancy by floor and weekday shows hybrid patterns without tracking individuals.

Does it integrate with the building management system?

Yes. Occupancy can be shared with HVAC and lighting controls so empty floors are not conditioned.

Can tenants see their own data?

Yes. Access can be limited by site and tenancy.

Can it measure meeting room use?

Yes. Sensors in meeting rooms measure actual occupancy, which can be compared with bookings to identify rooms that are booked but empty or the wrong size.

Does it track individual employees?

No. Counting is anonymous, with processing on the device and no images or personal information collected.
