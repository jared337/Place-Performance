Guide

Why the type of place matters.

A library, a stadium and a shared path all need people counting, but the questions, the sensors and the way they are installed are different. Each place page explains what is measured, how sensors are installed and which features matter most.

Indoor places

Libraries, campuses, shopping centres, office towers and government buildings typically use ceiling-mounted 3D counters at entrances and over key zones, with live floorplans showing occupancy room by room.

Outdoor places

Streets and laneways and parks and foreshores use sensors chosen for each environment to count pedestrians, cyclists and, where needed, vehicles. See parks, trails and streets.

Events and venues

Stadiums and venues combine gate counting with zone occupancy and capacity alerts, permanently or temporarily for a single event.

One platform for all of them

Whatever the mix, everything appears in one platform with consistent metrics, so a council can compare its libraries, parks and town centre side by side. Browse by sector on the solutions page.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What kinds of places can Place Performance measure?

Indoor places such as libraries, university buildings, shopping centres, offices and government buildings, and outdoor places such as streets, laneways, parks, foreshores, trails and event spaces.

Are different sensors used indoors and outdoors?

Yes. Indoors, 3D people counters are typically used at entrances and over zones. Outdoors, sensors are chosen for each environment to count pedestrians, cyclists and vehicles.

How is a sensor plan chosen for each place?

Every location starts with a site survey covering entrances, layout, ceiling heights, mounting options, power, connectivity and privacy needs. The sensor plan is then designed around the questions you want to answer.

Can temporary counting be used for events?

Yes. Temporary deployments suit festivals, markets and short studies, alongside permanent installations for ongoing monitoring.

Can one organisation measure several types of place?

Yes. All places appear in one platform with consistent metrics, so different kinds of site can be compared side by side.
