Measure how agency offices, service centres and public buildings are used, consolidate with confidence and report transparently.

Why measure

Public property, measured with public accountability in mind.

Government agencies manage offices, service centres, courts, cultural institutions and other public buildings. Each must be used efficiently, kept safe and reported on transparently, which all depends on knowing how many people use it.

Agency offices

Floor-by-floor occupancy over time shows average and peak use and identifies floors that are consistently under-used, supporting consolidation and workplace strategy. See office occupancy analytics for government.

Service centres and public buildings

Queue and wait-time monitoring at shopfronts measures service against targets, and live occupancy supports capacity limits and emergency management at public-facing sites. Read about real-time alerts.

Reporting and privacy

Board packs and scheduled reports go to executives and ministers automatically. Counting is anonymous and processed on the device, with role-based access and single sign-on, as described in privacy by design.

Typical uses in government

Property teams identify consolidation opportunities, workplace teams plan for hybrid attendance, service delivery teams monitor shopfront queues against targets, security teams use live occupancy for emergency management, and executives receive regular utilisation reporting.

How it is installed

How the platform works

  1. Survey. We assess entrances, ceiling heights, layout, lighting, power, network and privacy needs.
  2. Design. A sensor plan places FootfallCam 3D counters at entrances and over the zones you want to measure.
  3. Install and calibrate. Devices are installed, commissioned and checked for accuracy against manual counts.
  4. Support. Every device reports a heartbeat, and our team is alerted if one needs attention.

FAQ

Questions & answers

How can agencies measure office utilisation?

Sensors measure occupancy on every floor over time, showing average and peak utilisation by day and identifying under-used space.

Can it monitor service centre queues?

Yes. Queue sensors measure queue length and wait time against service targets, with alerts when targets are at risk.

Is the data anonymous?

Yes. Counting is processed on the device and no images or personal information are collected.

Where is the data hosted?

Hosting arrangements can be discussed to meet your agency's data and security requirements.

Can it support evacuation and emergency management?

Live occupancy shows how many people are in each building and floor at any moment, supporting capacity limits and emergency management.

Can reports go to executives automatically?

Yes. Scheduled reports and board packs summarise attendance, utilisation and trends without manual data gathering.
