Why measure

Footfall is the heartbeat of a centre.

Footfall tells centre managers whether marketing works, which entrances matter, how tenants perform and when to staff. Reliable counting at every entrance, plus sensors in key zones, turns that into a complete picture.

Entrances, malls and food courts

Entrance counters measure footfall by door and hour. Zone sensors show how visitors spread through malls, wings and food courts, and live occupancy helps cleaning and security respond to busy periods as they happen.

Sales and tenant performance

Integrating point-of-sale data turns footfall into conversion and sales per visitor, and zone ranking highlights strong and weak areas for leasing. Read more about footfall analytics for retail and precincts.

Campaigns and planning

Comparing a promotion with the expected baseline shows the uplift it created, and scenario planning tests changes such as extended trading hours. Outdoor counting extends the picture to surrounding streets and laneways.

Typical uses in centres

Centre managers report footfall to owners, leasing teams use zone data in tenant negotiations, marketing teams measure campaign uplift, operations teams roster cleaning and security to peaks, and asset managers compare centres across a portfolio.

FAQ

Questions & answers

How is footfall measured in a shopping centre?

3D people counters above each entrance count people in and out, and sensors in key zones measure occupancy and movement through the centre.

Can footfall be linked to sales?

Yes. Point-of-sale integrations combine visits with sales to calculate conversion and sales per visitor by zone.

Can it monitor food court queues?

Yes. Queue sensors measure queue length and wait time, with alerts when service targets are at risk.

Does footfall counting identify shoppers?

No. Counting is anonymous and processed on the device, with no images or personal information collected.

Can footfall be reported to owners automatically?

Yes. Scheduled reports deliver footfall, dwell and zone performance to owners and managers weekly, monthly or quarterly.

Can it compare centres in a portfolio?

Yes. Portfolio views rank centres and zones by footfall and performance and flag outliers.
