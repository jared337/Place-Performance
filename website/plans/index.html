Guide

Choosing the right plan level.

Place Performance plans follow a maturity path. Each level includes everything in the levels below it, so moving up never means replacing hardware or losing history. Most organisations choose a level based on the decisions they need to make today, then grow.

Essentials: know what is happening

Essentials covers visitor counting, entries and exits, live occupancy against capacity, peak periods, dwell time, dashboards, automated reports and device health. It suits a single library, community centre or shopping centre that needs reliable visitation numbers for reporting and day-to-day operations.

Insights: know why it is happening

Insights adds live floorplans, heat maps, visitor flow, zone comparison, repeat visitation and historical trends. It is the right level when you need to understand how people use space inside a building, for example study spaces on a campus or amenities on office floors. See these features on the real-time page.

Professional: improve how places perform

Professional adds multi-site portfolio views, alert rules with escalation, queue and operations analytics, integrations with point-of-sale, Wi-Fi and building management systems, the Place Performance Score and single sign-on. It suits councils and property owners running several sites.

Executive: lead with intelligence

Executive adds the performance index and benchmarking, AI insights, the Place Assistant, forecasting, scenario modelling, investment analysis and board reporting packs. It is designed for leadership teams making portfolio and capital decisions, such as government property rationalisation. Learn more on the AI and forecasting page.

What every plan includes

Every plan includes the sensors, site survey, installation, commissioning, the analytics platform, device health monitoring and ongoing support from our Australian team. Pricing depends on the number of sites and devices. The quickest way to find the right level is a pilot at one site, which you can arrange through the contact page.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What is included in every plan?

Every plan includes the sensors, site survey, installation, commissioning, the analytics platform, device health monitoring and ongoing support. Higher levels add capabilities on top of everything in the levels below.

How is Place Performance priced?

Pricing is based on the number of sites and devices and the capability level you choose. Contact us for a quote tailored to your locations.

Can we start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Many organisations start with Essentials at one or a few sites and move up a level when they are ready. Upgrades add features without replacing hardware or losing history.

Which level do we need for multiple sites?

Multi-site portfolio views, site ranking and outlier flags start at Professional (Level 3). Portfolio-wide performance index and external benchmarking are part of Executive (Level 4).

Which level includes AI and forecasting?

AI insights, the Place Assistant, forecasting, scenario modelling, investment analysis and board reporting packs are included in Executive (Level 4).

Can we run a pilot first?

Yes. A pilot at one site is the most common way to start. It proves accuracy and value with your own data before you commit to a wider rollout.
