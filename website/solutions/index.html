FAQ

Questions & answers

Which organisations use Place Performance?

It is designed for local councils, universities and TAFEs, state and federal government agencies, commercial property owners and managers, shopping centres and precinct operators, and managers of parks, trails and streets.

What problems does it solve?

The most common uses are crowd capacity and safety, space utilisation, portfolio rationalisation, demand-based operations such as cleaning and staffing, commercial performance, and measuring active transport and public spaces.

Can one organisation use it for several purposes?

Yes. The same sensors and data support safety, utilisation, operations and planning at the same time, and different teams see the views that matter to them.
