Guide

One measurement platform, many kinds of place.

Councils, universities, agencies, property owners and precinct managers ask different questions, but they all need the same thing first: accurate, anonymous numbers about how their places are used. Place Performance provides that foundation, then shapes dashboards, alerts and reports around each sector's decisions.

Choose by sector

Councils focus on crowd safety, service levels and evidence for funding. Universities measure space utilisation against bookings. Government agencies right-size office portfolios after the shift to hybrid work. Commercial property owners clean and condition space on demand. Shopping centres and precincts connect footfall to sales, and public-realm managers count pedestrians, cyclists and vehicles outdoors.

Choose by place

You can also start from the type of place you manage: libraries, university campuses, shopping centres, stadiums and venues, streets and laneways, parks and foreshores, office towers and government buildings. Each page explains what is measured there and how sensors are installed.

Shared foundations

Whatever the sector, every deployment uses the same platform: privacy-safe sensors, real-time occupancy and alerts, automated reporting and, at higher levels, forecasting and AI insights. Many organisations use one deployment for several purposes at once, such as a council measuring both library occupancy and shared-path cycling.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Which organisations use Place Performance?

It is designed for local councils, universities and TAFEs, state and federal government agencies, commercial property owners and managers, shopping centres and precinct operators, and managers of parks, trails and streets.

What problems does it solve?

The most common uses are crowd capacity and safety, space utilisation, portfolio rationalisation, demand-based operations such as cleaning and staffing, commercial performance, and measuring active transport and public spaces.

Which sector page should I start with?

Start with the sector that matches your organisation, such as councils or universities, or browse by the type of place you manage, such as libraries, campuses or parks.

Is the hardware the same in every sector?

The core technology is the same, but each deployment is designed for its environment. Indoor sites typically use FootfallCam 3D counters, while outdoor sites use sensors chosen for pedestrians, cyclists or vehicles.

Can one organisation use it for several purposes?

Yes. The same sensors and data support safety, utilisation, operations and planning at the same time, and different teams see the views that matter to them.
