FAQ

Questions & answers

Can you count cyclists and pedestrians on trails?

Yes. ITESA measures pedestrian and cyclist activity, including volumes and direction, on trails, shared paths and in parks, using technology selected for each outdoor environment.

Can it count vehicles?

Yes. Vehicle movement can be measured across roads, access points and car parks, including volumes and direction over time.

Can sensors be installed temporarily?

Yes. Temporary deployments are available for events and short studies, as well as permanent installations for ongoing monitoring.

Can it measure the impact of new infrastructure?

Yes. Measuring before, during and after a project shows how a new path, crossing or public space has changed how people use the area.

Do outdoor sensors need mains power and connectivity?

Power and connectivity are assessed for each location during design, and the technology is chosen to suit what is available, including remote environments.
