FAQ

Questions & answers

How can occupancy data reduce building operating costs?

Occupancy data lets you clean, heat, cool and light spaces based on actual use. Integrating it with a building management system means empty floors are not conditioned, and cleaning is triggered by visits rather than fixed schedules.

Does it integrate with building management systems?

Yes. Occupancy can be shared with building management systems such as HVAC and lighting controls, and integrations are configured for you.

Can tenants see their own data?

Yes. Access can be limited by site and tenancy, so tenants see occupancy and utilisation for their own space.

Can it prove the value of a refit?

Yes. Investment analysis compares occupancy, utilisation and dwell before and after a refit to show whether it achieved its goals.

How accurate are the sensors?

FootfallCam 3D counters are calibrated during commissioning, and continuous device health monitoring flags issues early so data stays reliable.
