Privacy by design

Measure the place, not the person.

Place Performance is built to understand how places are used without identifying who is there. Counting happens on the device, data is masked at source, and only anonymous numbers leave the site.

Principles

How privacy is built in.

Edge processing

Counting runs on the sensor itself. Video is not streamed or stored by the platform.

Masked at source

Data is anonymised on the device before anything is sent.

No personal information

No faces, names or identities are collected, only counts, occupancy and movement.

Minimal collection

The technology is chosen for the question, so only the data you need is measured.

Role-based access

Users see only the sites and features their role allows, with single sign-on support.

Audit trail

Administrative actions are logged for traceability and accountability.

Device level

Privacy settings you can verify.

Every device record shows its privacy mode and configuration. Demographic estimates are optional, anonymised and only used where hardware supports them and you choose to enable them.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Do the sensors record video?

No video is streamed to or stored by the platform. The sensors process what they see on the device and send only anonymous counts and occupancy numbers.

Can people be identified?

No. Data is masked at source and no personal information such as faces or names is collected. The system measures how places are used, not who uses them.

Does Place Performance use facial recognition?

No. Place Performance does not use facial recognition and does not identify individuals.

What data is collected?

Counts of people entering and leaving, occupancy, dwell time, movement between zones and, where enabled and supported, anonymous demographic estimates. Outdoors it can also count cyclists and vehicles.

Who can access the data?

Only users in your organisation with an assigned role. Access can be limited to specific sites, single sign-on can be enforced, and administrative actions are recorded in an audit log.

Does it support Australian privacy obligations?

Anonymous, edge-processed counting with minimal data collection is designed to support organisations' obligations under Australian privacy law. Contact us for documentation to support your privacy impact assessment.
