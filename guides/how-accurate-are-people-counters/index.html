How accurate are people counters?

Accuracy is the first question buyers ask. Here is what drives it, how it is measured, and how to keep counts reliable.

A people counter is only useful if you trust the number. Good systems are accurate, but accuracy is not a single fixed figure. It depends on the sensor, the installation and how the system is looked after.

What accuracy means

Accuracy is how closely the automated count matches the true number of people. Well-installed 3D and depth sensors commonly reach the high nineties in percentage terms, while simpler methods such as single beams or Wi-Fi estimates sit lower.

What affects accuracy

Placement and field of view, calibration, how busy the space is, groups and tailgating, and lighting all affect the result. A sensor that is well chosen for the site and carefully commissioned will hold accuracy that a poorly placed one never reaches.

How accuracy is measured

Accuracy is checked by comparing the sensor count against a known ground truth, usually a manual count over a set period, at the site itself. A figure proven in your own space matters far more than a number on a brochure.

Keeping accuracy high over time

Counts drift if a sensor is knocked, obscured or goes offline. Continuous device health monitoring flags problems early, and periodic validation confirms the numbers are still right, so the data stays dependable long after installation.

Questions & answers

How accurate is people counting?

Accuracy depends on the sensor and on how well it is installed and calibrated. Well-installed 3D and depth sensors commonly reach the high nineties in percentage terms, validated against a manual count on site, with device health monitoring to keep them reliable.

How is people counting accuracy tested?

By comparing the sensor count against a manual ground-truth count over a set period at the site itself, which proves accuracy in the real environment rather than in a lab.

Why do people counts drift over time?

A sensor can be knocked, obscured or go offline. Continuous device health monitoring catches these early and periodic validation confirms the counts are still accurate.
