Exhibitions & pop-ups

People counting for exhibitions, trade shows and pop-ups.

Measure attendance, entry flow and which stands and zones draw the crowd, for a single show or a touring pop-up, with temporary sensors that need no mains power or wiring.

Why measure

Know who came, and what drew them.

Exhibitions, trade shows and pop-ups live or die on attendance and engagement, but the space is temporary and often has no power or network. Place Performance deploys for the show, measures what matters, and packs up afterwards with the data in hand.

Attendance and entry flow

Counters at each entrance give a live attendance figure and arrival rate, so organisers can see how busy the floor is and when the peaks hit.

Stand and zone engagement

Zones drawn around stands, feature areas and activations measure how many people reach them and how long they dwell, so exhibitors and organisers can see what drew the crowd. The same approach powers marketing and display analytics.

Deployed for the event

Battery and solar sensors with 4G go in without mains power or cabling, measure for a day or a week, and are removed afterwards, ideal for touring pop-ups and one-off shows. See the hardware we use.

Typical uses at shows

Organisers report attendance and engagement to exhibitors and sponsors, exhibitors measure the draw of their stands, feature-zone owners compare activations, and operations teams watch entry flow and peak times to manage the floor.

How it is installed

From site survey to live data.

Our Australian team designs, installs and supports every deployment, so you deal with one partner from the first survey to ongoing maintenance.

How the platform works

  1. Plan. We map entrances, stands and feature zones, and agree what to measure before the show.
  2. Deploy. Battery, solar and 4G sensors are installed on site in hours, with no mains power or cabling.
  3. Measure live. Attendance and engagement are live throughout the event, on any device.
  4. Pack up and report. Sensors are removed afterwards and you keep the full data and reports.

FAQ

Questions & answers

Can you count a one-off show or a touring pop-up?

Yes. Temporary deployments use battery and solar sensors with 4G, installed for a single show or moved between venues on a tour, with no mains power or wiring needed.

What can we measure at an exhibition?

Live attendance and entry rate at the doors, plus footfall and dwell at individual stands, feature areas and activations, with peak times and day-by-day comparisons.

How quickly can it be set up on site?

Temporary sensors go in within hours, are tested against the floor plan, and are removed after the event.

Is it anonymous?

Yes. Counting is anonymous, processed on the device, with no images or personal information collected.

Can exhibitors see their own stand data?

Yes. A zone can be reported per stand or exhibitor, so each can see the footfall and dwell their space attracted.

Does it work without power or internet?

Yes. Battery and solar sensors with 4G run with no mains power or wired network, which suits temporary and touring deployments.
