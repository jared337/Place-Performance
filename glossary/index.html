Glossary

People counting and place analytics, defined

Plain-English definitions of the terms used across people counting, footfall and occupancy analytics.

People counting

People counting is the automated measurement of how many people enter, leave or occupy a space, using sensors instead of manual tallies. Modern systems count anonymously, without identifying anyone.

Footfall

Footfall is the number of people who visit a place over a period of time, such as a store, centre or street. Footfall analytics turns those counts into trends by entrance, hour, day and zone.

Occupancy analytics

Occupancy analytics measures how many people are in a space at a given moment and over time, so teams can manage capacity, safety, cleaning, energy and staffing against real demand.

Live occupancy

Live occupancy is the number of people currently inside a space, updated in near real time. It is often shown against a capacity limit, with alerts as the limit approaches.

Dwell time

Dwell time is how long people stay in a place or a particular zone. It helps separate a busy area that holds attention from one that people simply pass through.

Space utilisation

Space utilisation is how much a room, floor or building is actually used compared with its capacity, measured over time. It guides consolidation, scheduling and investment decisions.

Pedestrian counting

Pedestrian counting measures how many people move along a street, path or public space, often outdoors and sometimes alongside cyclist and vehicle counts, to inform planning and investment.

Heat map

In place analytics, a heat map shows where people concentrate within a space, so teams can see which zones, displays or routes attract and hold attention.

Crowd capacity monitoring

Crowd capacity monitoring tracks live occupancy against a safe or licensed limit for a venue or zone, with alerts before the limit is reached, to protect safety and experience.

Anonymous counting

Anonymous counting measures people without identifying them. Counting is processed on the device using shape, depth or movement, so no images or personal information leave the sensor.

LiDAR people counting

LiDAR people counting maps a space in three dimensions with pulses of light and counts people by shape and movement rather than by their faces. It works in any light and does not capture recognisable images.

Conversion rate

In retail analytics, conversion rate is the share of visitors who make a purchase, found by combining footfall with sales data. It shows whether visitation is turning into results.
