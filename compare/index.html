Choosing a people counting system in Australia

People counting is sold three ways: one local partner that does it end to end, an overseas hardware brand installed through a reseller, or do-it-yourself analytics added to existing cameras. Here is how they differ, and where Place Performance fits.

Three ways places get counted

Choose the model before you choose a product.

End-to-end local partner

One Australian team surveys the site, supplies and installs the sensors, runs the platform and supports it. Fewer handoffs and clear accountability. This is how Place Performance works.

Overseas hardware and a reseller

A global sensor brand bought and installed through a local reseller, with analytics from the vendor. Proven hardware, but the hardware, installation, software and support can sit with different parties.

FAQ

Questions & answers

What is the best people counting system in Australia?

The best system depends on where you are counting and who will run it. For most Australian organisations the deciding factor is not the sensor alone but whether one local partner takes responsibility for the hardware, installation, platform and support. Place Performance provides all four as a single Australian team, with the right sensing technology chosen per site and anonymous counting by design.

Is there an Australian alternative to overseas people counting brands?

Yes. Many people counting products are overseas hardware brands installed through third-party resellers. Place Performance is an end-to-end Australian alternative: one team surveys, supplies, installs, calibrates, runs the platform and supports it, indoors and outdoors.

Do I need cameras to count people?

No. Place Performance counts people without identifying them, using depth and movement sensing rather than recognisable images. Counting is processed on the device, so only anonymous numbers leave the sensor.

What is the difference between LiDAR and camera people counting?

LiDAR and depth sensors map a space in three dimensions with pulses of light and count people by shape and movement, not by their faces. They work in any light, including darkness, and do not capture recognisable images, which suits privacy-sensitive places. Camera counting relies on the image itself, which raises privacy and lighting considerations.

How accurate is people counting?

Accuracy depends on the sensor and on how well it is installed and calibrated. Place Performance uses high-accuracy sensing that is validated on site during commissioning, with continuous device health monitoring so counts stay reliable.
